# ATM_Performance_Analytics_Wisabi_Bank

### Banking Operations & Customer Transaction Analytics 

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Wisabi Bank's ATM transaction data for the period **January–December 2022** to evaluate ATM network performance, understand customer transaction behaviour and identify opportunities for operational improvement.

The analysis covers transaction volume, transaction value, ATM utilization, customer demographics, transaction duration and geographical performance across different Nigerian states.

Using **Power BI, DAX and data modelling**, the project transforms transaction data into interactive dashboards, performance indicators and actionable business insights.

## Business Problem

Wisabi Bank required better visibility into ATM usage patterns, customer transaction behaviour and operational performance across its ATM network.

The objective was to identify variations in ATM utilization, understand customer transaction preferences, evaluate transaction efficiency and highlight potential operational improvement opportunities.

### Key Business Questions

- Which geographical locations have the highest and lowest ATM utilization?
- How do transaction volumes and amounts vary across locations and time periods?
- Which transaction types are most frequently performed?
- How does transaction behaviour differ across customer age groups?
- What are the peak ATM transaction hours across different locations?
- How do average transaction amounts and processing durations vary by state and transaction type?
- What operational improvements can be recommended based on the observed patterns?

##  Key Business Findings

Analysis of the 2022 ATM transactions revealed:

- **2,143,838 transactions** were processed, with a total transaction value of approximately **₦38.56 billion**.
- **8,819 unique customers** performed at least one transaction.
- Overall ATM utilization was **12.9%**.
- Kano recorded the highest ATM utilization at **18.6%**, while FCT recorded the lowest at **8.5%**.
- Customers aged 15–25 recorded the highest transaction frequency.
- Withdrawals were the most common transaction type across age groups, accounting for more than 50% of transactions.
- ATM transaction activity peaked at different times across locations, with Lagos showing notable evening activity.
- March recorded the highest monthly transaction volume and transaction amount, while February recorded the lowest.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the observed performance patterns, the project proposed the following operational improvements:

**Improve ATM Utilization**
- Investigate comparatively low utilization in FCT.
- Improve ATM visibility and accessibility.
- Consider targeted promotions and additional ATM services.

**Improve Transaction Efficiency**
- Investigate longer transaction durations in Kano.
- Review ATM capacity, maintenance practices and potential transaction bottlenecks.
- Consider upgrading ATM infrastructure to improve transaction efficiency.

**Optimize ATM Operations**
- Schedule routine maintenance during periods of lower transaction activity.
- Maintain ATM availability during location-specific peak transaction periods.

**Promote Alternative Banking Channels**
- Encourage customers to use mobile and online banking for suitable transactions, including balance enquiries and transfers.
